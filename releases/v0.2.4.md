# Cheeky Foveated DLSS v0.2.4

- **Center supersampling:** adds a 1-2x slider to reconstruct the center at a higher resolution, then downsample it to its original size. Can improve center detail at the cost of additional GPU time and memory.
- **DX12 motion-vector fixes:** improves handling of the game's declared motion-vector layout for the center and peripheral DLAA, and resets center history after a skipped evaluation.
- **DLSS-NR history improvements:** preserves history during fixed-size crop movement and compensates motion vectors as the crop moves. NR remains experimental; instability during head movement is not fully resolved.
- **Easier bug reports:** adds a **Report an issue...** button that collects a support ZIP and opens a prefilled GitHub issue with diagnostics and settings. Review the report and attach the ZIP before submitting; nothing is uploaded automatically.

**Updating:** Close the game and replace `CheekyFoveatedDLSS.addon64`. If you're upgrading from a version older than v0.2.2 and use OpenXR, also close your OpenXR games and run the new `CheekyEyeTrackingSetup.exe`. The v0.2.2 and v0.2.3 OpenXR layers remain compatible and don't need updating. Native OpenVR games such as ACC need no OpenXR layer.
