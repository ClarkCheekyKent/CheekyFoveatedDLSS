**Cheeky Foveated DLSS v0.4.0**

- **UEVR AFW support (DX12):** adds foveated DLSS-SR and DLSS-NR compatibility with the public AFW UEVR build. NR supports Before/After upscaling, full-frame or foveated processing, and independent or SR-linked region settings.
- **AFW stereo and gaze coverage:** adds Automatic, Manual and Centered coverage modes that cover both eyes, with runtime eye tracking, simulated gaze and fixed fallback when tracking is unavailable. Start with **Stereo and gaze → AFW stereo coverage → Automatic** and use the alignment border to check both eyes.
- **AFW history stability:** preserves temporal history across alternate-eye crop positions and small gaze movements, while resetting affected SR/NR histories after skipped or failed passes. Returning from AFW to ordinary rendering resets skipped histories once before resuming normal processing.
- **Existing DX11/DX12 compatibility:** keeps native DLSS and Streamline processing working when the AFW runtime is loaded but Native Stereo or AFR is selected. Switching away from AFW restores ordinary stereo controls and calibration during the same session.
- **Stereo alignment stability:** keeps verified automatic alignment through stale gaze samples, slow NR frames and focus changes, preventing the foveated region from jumping back to manual offsets. Live eye tracking still requires fresh, focused gaze data.

**Updating:** Exit the game before replacing files:

- **ReShade:** Copy the new `CheekyFoveatedDLSS.addon64` over the existing add-on in the game's executable folder.
- **UEVR:** Unzip the complete UEVR package into the game's UEVR configuration directory, for example `%APPDATA%\UnrealVRMod\HogwartsLegacy`. Preserve the `plugins/` and `scripts/` layout and update both DLLs and the Lua script together. Your saved settings can stay in place. When moving from ReShade to UEVR, uninstall ReShade with its installer and remove the Cheeky add-on.

**OpenXR:** Run the matching `CheekyOpenXRSetup.exe` for ordinary OpenXR stereo alignment/calibration or OpenXR eye tracking. AFW fixed/automatic coverage and native OpenVR work without the OpenXR layer. DLSS-NR requires a compatible `nvngx_dlssnr.dll`, supplied separately.
